Installation instructions
  1. Unzip Flackey.zip.
  2. Run this in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Flackey.app

    The app is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate yet. This clears the download flag for the app you chose to install.

  3. Open Flackey and connect Telegram, Soulseek, or both.
