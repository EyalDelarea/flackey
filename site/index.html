How we verify audio

A FLAC label
isn’t proof.

Flackey looks inside every file before accepting it. A renamed or upscaled track doesn’t pass just because its filename says FLAC.

  1. 01

    Confirm the recording.

    When a Deezer preview is available, Chromaprint compares its audio fingerprint with the downloaded file to make sure it’s the same track and version.

  2. 02

    Inspect the spectrum.

    Flackey analyzes the frequencies in the audio to spot a lossy file disguised inside a lossless container.

  3. 03

    Enforce the quality floor.

    Fake lossless files and MP3s below 320 kbps are rejected. You get the best verified source Flackey can find.

About Flackey

We didn’t reinvent music sharing.
We made it easier.

Flackey is powered by Soulseek, an independent, community-powered network where music lovers share rare and high-quality recordings directly with one another. Flackey takes part in that exchange by sharing your downloaded tracks back with the network. When a lossless copy isn’t available, it falls back to a verified 320 kbps MP3 from Deezer.

We didn’t invent the wheel—we’re making these great sources more accessible. Flackey will grow around what its users actually ask for.

Installing Flackey
  1. Download and open Flackey.pkg.
  2. Follow the macOS Installer steps. Flackey is placed in Applications.
  3. Open Flackey and connect Telegram, Soulseek, or both.

Beta builds are not notarized yet. If macOS says the installer is from an unidentified developer, control-click it, choose Open, and confirm that you want to continue.
